# Machine Learning Documentation (`ML_DOCUMENTATION.md`)

## 1. Option A — Sales Forecasting
- **Models Evaluated**:
  1. **Gradient Boosting Regressor**: Additive stagewise regression trees capturing trend, quarterly residual structures, and short-term momentum.
  2. **Random Forest Regressor**: Ensemble regression over lag-1, lag-2, lag-3 rolling mean, and seasonal multipliers.
  3. **Linear Regression (Seasonal OLS)**: Ordinary least squares trend combined with Fourier seasonal harmonics.
- **Validation Protocol**: 80% chronological training split / 20% holdout testing split.
- **Evaluation Metrics**: MAE, RMSE, $R^2$, and MAPE (%).

## 2. Option B — Customer Segmentation (RFM K-Means++)
- **Features**:
  - **Recency ($R$)**: Days elapsed since the customer's most recent order.
  - **Frequency ($F$)**: Total number of distinct orders placed.
  - **Monetary ($M$)**: Total revenue generated by the customer.
- **Preprocessing**: Z-score standardization across $(R, F, M)$.
- **Evaluation**: Within-cluster sum of squares (Inertia) and exact **Silhouette Score**.
- **Dynamic Labeling**: Centroids are ranked by empirical composite value to label `High Value`, `Regular`, `Low Value`, and `At Risk` segments.

## 3. Option C — Anomaly Detection (Isolation Forest)
- **Features**: Multi-dimensional transaction extremity across `sales`, `quantity`, `discount`, and `profit_margin`.
- **Scoring**: Computes normalized path length and anomaly score $s(x, n) \in [0, 1]$ to isolate unusual bulk discounts and negative-margin orders.
